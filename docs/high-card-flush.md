# High Card Flush

Not really poker at all: a hand's rank is purely **how many cards of one suit** it holds. 52-card deck, reshuffled every round.

## How hands compare

You and the dealer each get **7 cards**. A longer flush always beats a shorter one, whatever the ranks. Flushes of the same length are compared card by card from the highest. Consecutive ranks make no difference here; only the Straight Flush bonus cares about that.

## Bets and decisions

Place an **Ante**, plus optional **Flush**, **Straight Flush** and **Jackpot** side bets. After seeing your 7 cards:

- **Fold**: lose the Ante
- **Raise**: normally 1x the Ante, up to **2x** with a 5-card flush, up to **3x** with a 6 or 7-card flush

Clicking a card moves it into the "YOUR FLUSH" area, but that's just for show. You're always paid on your true best flush.

## Main game

The dealer qualifies with a **3-card, 9-high flush** or better (any 4+ card flush always qualifies).

| Result | Ante | Raise |
|---|---|---|
| Dealer doesn't qualify | Pays 1:1 | Push |
| You win | Pays 1:1 | Pays 1:1 |
| Dealer wins | Lost | Lost |
| Tie | Push | Push |

## Flush and Straight Flush bonuses

Judged on your own hand, whatever happens in the main game (including a fold).

| Length | Flush Bonus | Straight Flush Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| 7 cards | 250:1 | 500:1 |
| 6 cards | 100:1 | 200:1 |
| 5 cards | 10:1 | 100:1 |
| 4 cards | 1:1 | 60:1 |
| 3 cards | - | 8:1 |

## Jackpot (£1)

Judged on your **straight flush** length (not plain flush length). Still pays if you fold.

| Straight flush length | Pays |
|---|---|
| 7 cards | **100% of the pool** |
| 6 cards | **50% of the pool** (doesn't reset it) |
| 5 cards | £250 |
| 4 cards | £50 |
| 3 cards | £5 |
