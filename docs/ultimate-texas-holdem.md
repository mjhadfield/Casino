# Ultimate Texas Hold'em

Head-to-head Hold'em against the dealer. 52-card deck (no Joker), reshuffled every round.

## Bets

**Ante** and **Blind** are always equal. **Trips** and **Jackpot** are optional. You need at least **3x your Ante** to deal.

## How a round works

You and the dealer each get 2 cards, and 5 community cards are dealt face down and shared by both hands. You get up to three chances to bet into **Play**:

| When | Options |
|---|---|
| Pre-flop (your 2 cards only) | Bet **4x** or **3x** your Ante, or Check. The flop is revealed |
| After the flop | Bet **2x**, or Check. The turn is revealed |
| After the turn | Bet **1x**, or **Fold** (losing Ante, Blind and Trips). The river is revealed |

Once you've bet, the rest of the community cards and the dealer's hand are revealed and the round settles.

## Main game

Each hand is the best 5 from your own 2 cards plus the 5 community cards.

- The dealer needs a **Pair or better** to qualify. If they don't, the **Ante pushes**, but Play and Blind still settle against the dealer's hand
- Beat the dealer: Ante and Play pay **1:1**
- The **Blind** only pays (see below) if you win with a **Straight or better**. Winning with anything less pushes it

## Trips and Blind payouts

| Hand | Trips | Blind* |
|---|---|---|
| Royal Flush | 50:1 | 500:1 |
| Straight Flush | 40:1 | 50:1 |
| Four of a Kind | 20:1 | 10:1 |
| Full House | 7:1 | 3:1 |
| Flush | 6:1 | 3:2 |
| Straight | 5:1 | 1:1 |
| Three of a Kind | 3:1 | - |

\*Blind only pays on these hands if you also beat the dealer.

**Trips** is judged on your own best hand alone, whatever the dealer has, and is lost if you fold.

## Jackpot (£1)

Judged on a different hand from everything else: your 2 cards **plus the flop only**, fixed the moment the flop is revealed. Never pays on a folded round.

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Royal Flush | **100% of the pool** |
| Straight Flush | **10% of the pool** (doesn't reset it) |
| Four of a Kind | £300 |
| Full House | £50 |
| Flush | £40 |
| Straight | £30 |
| Three of a Kind | £9 |
