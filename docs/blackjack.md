# Blackjack

8-deck shoe, up to 2 boxes played side by side (with identical bets on each), and four side bets.

## Tables

There are three Blackjack tables. They all play the same rules below, except:

| Table | Difference | Story mode unlock |
|---|---|---|
| **6:5** | A natural Blackjack pays **6:5** instead of 3:2. A real-world "worse for the player" table, and you should walk away from one in real life | Play 20 hands of Three Card Poker |
| **Standard** | Blackjack pays **3:2**, as it should | Play 100 hands of Blackjack |
| **Counting** | The shoe is **not** reshuffled every round. It's dealt down until 75% has gone (the cut card is shown in the shoe), and the running **Hi-Lo count** is shown in the top bar, updating card by card | Play 500 hands of Blackjack |

The Standard and 6:5 tables reshuffle the shoe every round. All three tables share one set of stats and achievements.

## Main game

- **Dealer peek**: an Ace or 10-value up-card checks the hole card straight away. A dealer Blackjack ends the round before anyone can Hit, Double or Split into it
- **Insurance**: offered on an Ace up-card only, up to half a box's bet, pays **2:1**
- **Double** on any first two cards, including after a split
- **Split** any equal-*value* pair (so J+Q counts), up to 4 times (5 hands). Split Aces get one card each and stand automatically, with no re-splitting
- A 21 on a split hand is just 21, not a Blackjack
- The dealer **stands on all 17s** (soft and hard), and always finishes their hand once every box is done
- Blackjack pays **3:2** (6:5 on the 6:5 table). Otherwise a win pays **1:1**, a tie pushes and a bust loses

## Side bets

All side bets use the box's first two cards plus the dealer's up-card as a 3-card poker hand (ranked like [Three Card Poker](three-card-poker.md#hand-ranking)), except Super Pairs.

### Super Pairs

Your first two cards only (plus the dealer's up-card for Suited Trips).

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Suited Trips (your pair *and* the dealer's up-card, same rank and suit) | 50:1 |
| Suited Pair | 25:1 |
| Prime Pair (same colour, different suits) | 10:1 |
| Any Pair | 5:1 |

### 21+3

Flush or better pays a flat **9:1**.

### Top 3

Only playable alongside a 21+3 bet.

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Three of a Kind, suited | 270:1 |
| Straight Flush | 180:1 |
| Three of a Kind | 90:1 |

### Jackpot (£1)

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Suited Three of a Kind, Aces / Kings / Queens | **100% of the pool** (split between boxes if both hit) |
| Suited Three of a Kind, any other rank | £625 |
| Straight Flush | £125 |
| Three of a Kind, off-suit | £100 |
| Straight | £30 |
| Flush | £10 |

Bought from the Crowns Store (200 Crowns) and switched on in Settings. A strip under the table shows the basic-strategy chart row for your current hand against every dealer up-card, with the right play for this round highlighted.
