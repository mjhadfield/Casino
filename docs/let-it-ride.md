# Let It Ride

52-card deck (no Joker), reshuffled every round.

## Bets

Three equal starter bets, labelled **£**, **2** and **1**. Optional side bets: **Bonus** (flat £1), **3 Card Bonus** (any amount) and **Jackpot** (flat £1).

## How a round works

You get 3 cards. The dealer's 2 cards are dealt face down and act as community cards.

1. **First decision** (your 3 cards): **Pull Back** bet "1" (you get it back) or **Let It Ride**. The first community card is revealed
2. **Second decision** (4 of your 5 cards known): Pull Back bet "2" or Let It Ride. The second community card is revealed

The **£** bet always plays.

## Payouts

You need a **Pair of Tens or better** to win. A lower pair or worse just loses, there's no push. Every starter bet still in play pays separately.

| Hand | Base Game | Bonus* | 3 Card** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Royal Flush | 500:1 | 10000:1 | 40:1 |
| Straight Flush | 100:1 | 2000:1 | 40:1 |
| Four of a Kind | 25:1 | 400:1 | - |
| Full House | 15:1 | 200:1 | - |
| Flush | 10:1 | 50:1 | 4:1 |
| Straight | 5:1 | 25:1 | 6:1 |
| Three of a Kind | 3:1 | 5:1 | 30:1 |
| Two Pair | 2:1 | - | - |
| Pair of Tens or better | 1:1 | - | - |
| Any Pair | - | - | 1:1 |

\*Bonus uses the same final 5-card hand but needs Three of a Kind or better, so Two Pair wins the Base Game but loses the Bonus.
\*\*3 Card uses your own 3 cards only. A-K-Q suited pays the same as any other Straight Flush.

Bonus and 3 Card don't care whether you pulled bets back.

## Jackpot (£1)

Uses the same final 5-card hand.

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Royal Flush | **100% of the pool** |
| Straight Flush | **10% of the pool** (doesn't reset it) |
| Four of a Kind | £300 |
| Full House | £50 |
| Flush | £40 |
| Straight | £30 |
| Three of a Kind | £9 |
