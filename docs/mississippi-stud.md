# Mississippi Stud

52-card deck (no Joker), reshuffled every round.

## How a round works

You get 2 cards face up, and 3 community cards are dealt face down. At **3rd, 4th and 5th Street** you either:

- **Bet 1x to 3x your Ante** (which reveals that street's community card), or
- **Fold**, losing the Ante and any street bets already made

You need at least **3x your Ante** to deal. A 2x or 3x bet on 3rd or 4th Street is only offered if you could still afford a 1x bet on the next street.

## Main game

Your hand is your 2 cards plus the 3 community cards. Every bet still in play (Ante plus each street you bet) pays the same odds:

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Royal Flush | 500:1 |
| Straight Flush | 100:1 |
| Four of a Kind | 40:1 |
| Full House | 10:1 |
| Flush | 6:1 |
| Straight | 4:1 |
| Three of a Kind | 3:1 |
| Two Pair | 2:1 |
| Pair, Jacks or better | 1:1 |
| Pair, 6s to 10s | Push |
| Pair, 2s to 5s, or High Card | Lose |

## 3 Card Bonus

Judged on the 3 community cards alone, whatever your own hand. It stays in play if you fold (the remaining cards are revealed to settle it).

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Mini-Royal (A-K-Q suited) | 50:1 |
| Straight Flush | 40:1 |
| Three of a Kind | 30:1 |
| Straight | 6:1 |
| Flush | 3:1 |
| Pair | 1:1 |

## Jackpot (£1)

Never pays on a folded round.

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Royal Flush | **100% of the pool** |
| Straight Flush | **10% of the pool** (doesn't reset it) |
| Four of a Kind | £300 |
| Full House | £50 |
| Flush | £40 |
| Straight | £30 |
| Three of a Kind | £9 |
