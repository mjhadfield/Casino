# Pai Gow Poker

53-card deck (52 + one Joker), reshuffled every round. There are two tables: **Standard** (the "Fortune" variant) and **Face Up!**.

## Setting your hand

You and the dealer each get 7 cards. You split yours into a 2-card **Front** hand and a 5-card **Back** hand. The Back must rank higher than the Front, so the Set button stays disabled until it does.

The dealer always sets their hand using the Casino Real **House Way** chart. On the Standard table, the **House Way** button sets yours the same way with one click.

**The Joker is semi-wild.** It can only complete a Straight, Flush or Straight Flush. Otherwise it counts as an Ace, and it can never stand in for a random card to make a pair, trips or full house.

## Main game (Ante)

| Result | Ante |
|---|---|
| Win both hands | Pays **1:1**, less **5% commission** on the win |
| Win one, lose one | Push |
| Lose both | Lost |

A tied hand (a "copy") goes to the dealer.

## Fortune side bet

Uses the best hand from your own 7 cards.

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| 7-Card Straight Flush | 5000:1 |
| Royal Flush + Royal Match (Royal Flush with K-Q suited as the other 2 cards) | 2000:1 |
| 7-Card Straight Flush with the Joker | 1000:1 |
| Five Aces | 400:1 |
| Royal Flush | 150:1 |
| Straight Flush | 50:1 |
| Four of a Kind | 25:1 |
| Full House | 5:1 |
| Flush | 4:1 |
| Three of a Kind | 3:1 |
| Straight | 2:1 |

## Jackpot (£1)

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| 7-Card Straight Flush | **100% of the pool** |
| Royal Flush + Royal Match | **50% of the pool** |
| 7-Card Straight Flush with the Joker | **25% of the pool** |
| Five Aces | £2,500 |
| Royal Flush | £200 |
| Straight Flush | £100 |
| Four of a Kind | £75 |
| Full House | £6 |

## Face Up! table

Same deck, Joker rules, Fortune and Jackpot, with four differences:

- **The dealer's hand is set and shown first**, before you set your own
- **Ace-high pushes.** If the dealer's 7 cards make nothing (no pair, straight or flush, Ace high), the round ends straight away: the Ante pushes, and Fortune and Jackpot still pay as normal
- **No commission.** A win pays a flat 1:1
- **Fold instead of House Way.** You can already see whether the dealer is beatable, so the House Way button is replaced by **Fold**: give up the Ante without setting a hand. Fortune and Jackpot still pay as normal

In story mode, Face Up! unlocks first (20 hands of Blackjack) and Standard after 100 hands of Pai Gow.

## Hand ranking

Standard 5-card poker:

`Straight Flush > Four of a Kind > Full House > Flush > Straight > Three of a Kind > Two Pair > One Pair > High Card`
