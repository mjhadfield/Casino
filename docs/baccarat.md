# Baccarat

8-deck shoe, reshuffled every round. No decisions after you bet, and no progressive jackpot.

## Card values

Ace = 1, 2 to 9 = face value, 10/J/Q/K = 0. A hand's total is the last digit of the sum (so 7 + 8 = 5).

## Drawing rules

A **natural** (two-card total of 8 or 9) on either side ends the round straight away.

Otherwise the **Player** draws a third card on 0 to 5 and stands on 6 or 7.

The **Banker** draws on 0 to 5 if the Player stood. If the Player drew, it depends on the Player's third card:

| Banker total | Banker draws when the Player's 3rd card is |
|---|---|
| 0, 1, 2 | always |
| 3 | anything except 8 |
| 4 | 2 to 7 |
| 5 | 4 to 7 |
| 6 | 6 or 7 |
| 7 | never |

## Main bets

| Bet | Pays |
|---|---|
| Player | 1:1 |
| Banker | 1:1, less 5% commission (£10 returns £19.50) |
| Tie | 8:1. Loses outright if it isn't a tie |

Player and Banker push on a tie.

## Dragon Bonus

Player Dragon and Banker Dragon are separate spots, each judged on the side you backed.

- Your side loses: lose
- Natural tie: push. Any other tie: lose
- Natural win: 1:1, whatever the margin
- Non-natural win: paid by margin of victory

| Win by | Pays |
|---|---|
| 9 | 30:1 |
| 8 | 10:1 |
| 7 | 6:1 |
| 6 | 4:1 |
| 5 | 2:1 |
| 4 | 1:1 |
| 1 to 3 | Lose |

## 5 Treasures

Five separate spots, each paid on what happened in the round, whatever the main result.

| Bet | Needs | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| Fortune 7 | Banker 3-card total of 7 | 40:1 |
| Golden 8 | Player 3-card total of 8 | 25:1 |
| Heavenly 9 | Both sides 3-card total of 9 | 75:1 |
| Heavenly 9 | One side 3-card total of 9 | 10:1 |
| Blazing 7's | Both sides 3-card total of 7 | 200:1 |
| Blazing 7's | Both sides 2-card total of 7 (neither drew) | 50:1 |
| Cover All | Any of the four events above happened | 6:1 |

Cover All pays when any of the other events happens, even if you didn't bet on that event's own spot.
