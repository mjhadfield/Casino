# Three Card Poker

Played with UK casino payout rules. This is the one table that's open from the start in story mode.

## Bets

| Bet | Notes |
|---|---|
| Ante | Required. Doubled into **Play** if you play your hand |
| Pair Plus | Optional side bet on your own hand |
| Prime | Optional side bet on suit colour (UK variation) |
| Jackpot | Optional, flat £1, shared progressive pool |

## Main game (Ante & Play)

You see your 3 cards, then either **Play** (match your Ante) or **Fold** (lose the Ante).

- The dealer qualifies with **Queen-high** or better
- Dealer doesn't qualify: Ante pays **1:1**, Play **pushes**
- You beat a qualifying dealer: Ante and Play both pay **1:1**
- Dealer beats you: Ante and Play are both lost
- Tie: both push

## Ante Bonus

Paid on your hand whatever the dealer has, but lost if you fold.

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Straight Flush | 5:1 |
| Three of a Kind | 4:1 |
| Straight | 1:1 |

## Pair Plus

Judged on your hand alone. Still pays if you fold.

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Straight Flush | 35:1 |
| Three of a Kind | 33:1 |
| Straight | 6:1 |
| Flush | 4:1 |
| Pair | 1:1 |

## Prime

Based on suit colour.

| Result | Pays |
|---|---|
| All 6 cards (yours + dealer's) the same colour | 4:1 |
| All 3 of your cards the same colour | 3:1 |

The 6-card result replaces the 3-card one rather than stacking with it.

## Jackpot (£1)

| Hand | Pays |
|---|---|
| Royal Flush in spades | **100% of the pool** |
| Royal Flush, any other suit | £500 |
| Straight Flush | £100 |
| Three of a Kind | £60 |
| Straight | £6 |

## Hand ranking

In 3-card poker a Straight beats a Flush (a 3-card straight is rarer than a 3-card flush):

`Straight Flush > Three of a Kind > Straight > Flush > Pair > High Card`
